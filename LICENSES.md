## Downloaded on first run (not in the installer)

When you click **Install** in Setup, the app downloads these to your computer
(`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Kirigami`). They are not part of the installer and keep their own licenses:

- **Python 3.12** ([python-build-standalone](https://github.com/astral-sh/python-build-standalone)
  builds of CPython, Python Software Foundation License and the licenses of the bundled libraries).
- The card maker's Python packages from [PyPI](https://pypi.org): click, cloudscraper, filetype,
  matplotlib, mtg_parser, natsort, numpy, pillow, pydantic, pypdfium2 and requests (the versions pinned
  in `requirements.txt`), plus the packages they depend on. Maintainer-only tools listed in
  `requirements.txt` (ezdxf, pyautogui, pywinauto, split-image) are not installed.

## Used if you installed them (never shipped)

- **Upscayl** ([upscayl.org](https://upscayl.org), AGPL-3.0). The app only detects and runs the copy you
  installed yourself. Upscayl's "Remacri", "Ultramix" and "Ultrasharp" models are marked
  Non-Commercial by Upscayl; the app shows that label next to them.
- **Silhouette Studio** (Silhouette America). The app opens the cutting templates with it if it is
  installed.
